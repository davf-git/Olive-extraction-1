[**Playing for Change**](https://playingforchange.com/) is a [multimedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multimedia "Multimedia") music project, created by the American producer and sound engineer Mark Johnson with his Timeless Media Group, that seeks to inspire, connect, and bring peace to the world through music. Playing For Change also created a separate [non-profit organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-profit_organization "Non-profit organization") called the Playing For Change Foundation, which builds music schools for children around the world. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Playing_for_Change)

[**PLAYING FOR CHANGE FOUNDATION**](https://playingforchange.org/)

Playing For Change was born in 2002 as a shared vision between co-founders, Mark Johnson, Raan Williams and [Whitney Kroenke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitney_Kroenke_Burditt "Whitney Kroenke Burditt"), to hit the streets of America with a mobile recording studio and cameras in search of inspiration and the heartbeat of the people. Producers Johnson and Enzo Buono traveled around the world to places including [New Orleans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans "New Orleans"), [Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barcelona "Barcelona"), [South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa "South Africa"), [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India "India"), [Nepal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepal "Nepal"), the [Middle East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_East "Middle East") and [Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ireland "Ireland"). Using mobile recording equipment, the duo recorded local musicians performing the same song, interpreted in their own style. Among the artists participating or openly involved in the project are [Vusi Mahlasela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vusi_Mahlasela "Vusi Mahlasela"), [Louis Mhlanga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Mhlanga "Louis Mhlanga"), [Clarence Bekker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clarence_Bekker "Clarence Bekker"), [David Guido Pietroni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Guido_Pietroni "David Guido Pietroni"), [Tal Ben Ari (Tula)](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Tal_Ben_Ari_%28Tula%29&action=edit&redlink=1 "Tal Ben Ari (Tula) (page does not exist)"), [Bono](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bono "Bono"), [Keb' Mo'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keb%27_Mo%27 "Keb"), [David Broza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Broza "David Broza"), [Manu Chao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manu_Chao "Manu Chao"), [Grandpa Elliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grandpa_Elliott "Grandpa Elliott"), [Keith Richards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_Richards "Keith Richards"), Toots Hibbert from [Toots & the Maytals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toots_%26_the_Maytals "Toots & the Maytals"), [Taj Mahal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taj_Mahal_%28musician%29 "Taj Mahal (musician)") and [Stephen Marley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Marley_%28musician%29 "Stephen Marley (musician)"). This resulted in the award-winning documentary *A Cinematic Discovery of Street Musicians*.

In 2005, Mark Johnson was walking in [Santa Monica, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Monica,_California "Santa Monica, California"), when he heard the voice of Roger Ridley (now deceased) singing 'Stand By Me.' [[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Playing_for_Change#cite_note-1) Roger had so much soul and conviction in his voice, and Mark approached him about performing 'Stand By Me' as a Song Around The World. Roger agreed, and when Mark returned with recording equipment and cameras he asked Roger, 'With a voice like yours, why are you singing on the streets?' Roger replied, 'Man I’m in the Joy business, I come out to be with the people.' Ever since that day, the Playing For Change crew has traveled the world recording and filming musicians, creating Songs Around The World, and building a global family.

Creating Songs Around The World inspired the PFC crew to unite many of the greatest musicians they met throughout their journey through the creation of the Playing For Change Band. These musicians come from many different countries and cultures, but through music they speak the same language. The PFC Band is now touring the world and spreading the message of love and hope to audiences everywhere.

The founders of Playing For Change created the Playing For Change Foundation, a 501(c)3 nonprofit organization dedicated to building music and art schools for children around the world, and creating hope and inspiration for the future of our planet.
