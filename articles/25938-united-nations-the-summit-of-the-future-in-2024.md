# **The Summit of the Future in 2024**

### **Multilateral solutions for a better tomorrow**

Major global shocks in recent years – including the [COVID-19](https://www.un.org/en/coronavirus) pandemic, the [Ukraine war](https://news.un.org/en/tags/ukraine), and the triple planetary crisis, among others – have challenged our international institutions. Unity around our shared principles and common goals is both crucial and urgent.

The Summit of the Future is a once-in-a-generation opportunity to enhance cooperation on critical challenges and address gaps in global governance, reaffirm existing commitments including to the [Sustainable Development Goals](https://www.un.org/en/common-agenda/sustainable-development-goals) (SDGs) and the [United Nations Charter](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/un-charter), and move towards a reinvigorated multilateral system that is better positioned to positively impact people’s lives. Building on the SDG Summit in 2023, [Member States](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/member-states) will consider ways to lay the foundations for more effective global cooperation that can deal with today’s challenges as well as new threats in the future.
