The [2020 WSIS Forum](https://www.itu.int/net4/wsis/forum/2020/), co-organized by ITU, UNESCO, UNDP and UNCTAD, is a collaborative event aiming at fostering partnerships, information exchange, creation of knowledge and sharing of best practices on Information and Communication Technology (ICT). The online event will be ongoing until 31 August 2020.

This picture of a child learning online during the Coronavirus quarantine was taken by [Magnum partner Gregory Halpern](https://pro.magnumphotos.com/CS.aspx?VP3=SearchResult&VBID=2K1HZOB5K6EO_F) in Rochester, USA, on 26 April 2020.
