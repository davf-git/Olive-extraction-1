# **Curious Kids Live: join us to learn about rainforests**

January 28, 2021 1.55pm GMT

Author

**![](images/25649/02.jpg)**

[Grace Allen -](https://theconversation.com/uk/team#grace-allen) Cities, Education and Young People Editor

### Where in the world do you find rainforests? What are the weird and wonderful creatures that live there? Is the rainforest under threat? And what about people – is the rainforest their home too?

The Conversation’s series Curious Kids responds to children’s questions on all sorts of topics – ranging from [why dogs are so cute](https://theconversation.com/curious-kids-why-is-our-dog-so-cute-138035) to [how much a cloud weighs](https://theconversation.com/curious-kids-how-much-does-a-cloud-weigh-143215). Now, we’re going live. Join us online to get answers from experts on a subject primary school kids may cover as part of their curriculum: the rainforest.

We’ll begin by talking about why rainforests are important, how they can be looked after, and who – and what – lives there. Then we’ll get into answering as many questions from children as we can, so send your queries to us at [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection), or tweet us @ConversationUK with #curiouskids.

The Conversation’s Curious Kids editor Grace Allen will host the online discussion at 2pm GMT on February 4, with:

[Louise Gentle](https://theconversation.com/profiles/louise-gentle-161525), Senior Lecturer in Wildlife Conservation at Nottingham Trent University

[Liana Chua](https://theconversation.com/profiles/liana-chua-183219), Reader in Anthropology at Brunel University London.

You can watch the webinar live on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/148546898646992/posts/1764680700366929/), [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=08CpDInjzAM) or [Twitter](https://twitter.com/ConversationUK?ref_src=twsrc%5Egoogle%7Ctwcamp%5Eserp%7Ctwgr%5Eauthor) from 2pm GMT on February 4.
