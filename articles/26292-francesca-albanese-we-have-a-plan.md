### **Francesca Albanese: We have a plan!**

**WIKIPEDIA: Francesca Paola Albanese** (Italian: [[franˈtʃeska ˈpaːola albaˈneːze, -eːse]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/Italian "Help:IPA/Italian"); born 30 March 1977) is an Italian legal scholar and expert on [human rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_rights "Human rights"). She has served as the [United Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations "United Nations") (UN) [Special Rapporteur on the occupied Palestinian territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UN_Special_Rapporteur_on_the_occupied_Palestinian_territories "UN Special Rapporteur on the occupied Palestinian territories") since 1 May 2022; initially appointed for a three-year term, she was confirmed for another three years in April 2025. She is the first woman to hold the position.

As part of her position as a UN special rapporteur, Albanese has been critical of [Israel's occupation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli-occupied_territories "Israeli-occupied territories") of the [Palestinian territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_territories "Palestinian territories") and recommended in her first report that [UN member states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UN_member_states "UN member states") develop a plan to end the occupation and [apartheid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_and_apartheid "Israel and apartheid"). After the [Israeli invasion of the Gaza Strip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_invasion_of_the_Gaza_Strip "Israeli invasion of the Gaza Strip"), Albanese [called for an immediate ceasefire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calls_for_a_ceasefire_during_the_Gaza_war "Calls for a ceasefire during the Gaza war") and warned that Palestinians in Gaza were at risk of [ethnic cleansing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnic_cleansing "Ethnic cleansing"). In March 2024, Albanese reported to the [UN Human Rights Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UN_Human_Rights_Council "UN Human Rights Council") that [Israel's actions in Gaza amounted to genocide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_genocide "Gaza genocide"). Pro-Israel organizations, including the US Government, have accused Albanese of [antisemitism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antisemitism "Antisemitism") and [anti-Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Israel "Anti-Israel") bias, and have called for her removal. Several human rights groups and numerous scholars of antisemitism have said the accusations are [illegitimate attempts to discredit her](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weaponization_of_antisemitism "Weaponization of antisemitism"). The UN published a report by Albanese in June 2025 stating that many corporate entities, including [Microsoft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft "Microsoft"), [Alphabet Inc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alphabet_Inc. "Alphabet Inc."), and [Amazon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_(company) "Amazon (company)"), were enabling and profiting from the occupation of Palestinian territories and the Gaza genocide. In response, the United States under the [Trump administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_presidency_of_Donald_Trump "Second presidency of Donald Trump") imposed sanctions on Albanese under [Executive Order 14203](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_Order_14203 "Executive Order 14203") naming her a '[specially designated national](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Specially_Designated_Nationals_and_Blocked_Persons_List "Specially Designated Nationals and Blocked Persons List")', forbidding all US persons and companies from doing business with her.

Albanese holds a [law degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_degree "Law degree") with honours from the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa "University of Pisa") and a [Master of Laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Laws "Master of Laws") in [human rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_rights "Human rights") from [SOAS University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SOAS_University_of_London "SOAS University of London"). She was an affiliate scholar at the [Institute for the Study of International Migration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_the_Study_of_International_Migration "Institute for the Study of International Migration") at [Georgetown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgetown_University "Georgetown University") until late 2025, when the University stated it was ended due to the US sanctions. She is a senior advisor on Migration and Forced Displacement at the non-profit [Arab Renaissance for Democracy and Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_Renaissance_for_Democracy_and_Development "Arab Renaissance for Democracy and Development").
