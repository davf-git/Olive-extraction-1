# Mia Mottley, Prime Minister of Barbados at the Opening of the [#COP27](https://www.youtube.com/hashtag/cop27) World Leaders Summit

Mia Mottley, Prime Minister of Barbados, makes a speech at the Opening of the [#COP27](https://www.youtube.com/hashtag/cop27) World Leaders Summit of the United Nations Climate Change Conference (COP27) - full length version.

At COP 27, countries come together to take action towards achieving the world’s collective climate goals as agreed under the Paris Agreement and the Convention. Building on the outcomes and momentum of COP 26 in Glasgow last year, nations are expected to demonstrate at COP 27 that they are in a new era of implementation by turning their commitments under The Paris Agreement into action.
