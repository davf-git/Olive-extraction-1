Beautiful Words - simple as that! (ON Ed)

*we have*

*demonstrated that it*

*is possible for people*

*coming from all lands*

*and climes of the*

*world...to live together*

*as good neighbours, in*

*peace and harmony. if*

*we can do it here, it can*

*be done elsewhere*

Fiorello la Guardia 1938

Sabine Hornig, *La Guardia Vistas* (2020). Commissioned by LaGuardia Gateway Partners in partnership with Public Art Fund for LaGuardia Airport’s Terminal B.
