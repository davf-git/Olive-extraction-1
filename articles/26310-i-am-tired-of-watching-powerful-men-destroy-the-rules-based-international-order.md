Abir Al Sahlani adressing the European Parliament: Abir Al-Sahlani is an Iraqi-born Swedish Member of the

European Parliament and since July 2019 a member of the European Parliament, representing the Centre Party.

# 

# **I am tired of watching powerful men destroy the rules-based international order**

# **and then ask the rest of the world to accept the consequences.**

### Wars are started. Occupations are upheld. Humanitarian aid is weaponised. International law is ignored.

### And the price is paid by women, children, persons with disabilities and civilians who never chose these conflicts.

### Peace is not built by statements.

### Peace is built when powerful people finally face consequences.

### 

### Al Sahlani was born in [Basra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basra), [Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraq) in 1976[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abir_Al-Sahlani#cite_note-4) to a middle class Iraqi family. Her father, Faisal Sahlani, was a leading

### opposition politician. He was a member of the [Communist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_Communist_Party), which at that time, was banned by [Saddam Hussein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saddam_Hussein)'s

### [Ba'athist regime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ba%27athist_Iraq). She left Iraq with her mother, fleeing the brutality of the [former regime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ba%27athist_Iraq), leaving behind her father,

### a political prisoner in [Abu Ghraib prison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Ghraib_prison).[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abir_Al-Sahlani#cite_note-5) Al Sahlani arrived in Sweden alone at the age of 15 and began her political

### career soon afterwards.[[6] - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abir_Al-Sahlani#cite_note-6)
