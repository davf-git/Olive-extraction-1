[**The Morrab Botanical Gardens**](http://www.morrabgardens.org/about-the-gardens/) are a sub tropical garden in the heart of Penzance and home to the rare and remarkable Morrab Library.  Nestled in the grounds sits a partially-derelict, former stables building...

The Hypatia Trust is working together with [**The Friends of Morrab Gardens**](http://www.morrabgardens.org/the-friends-of-morrab-gardens/) and the [**Pengarth Day Centre**](http://www.pengarth.co.uk/)to revitalise the old stables that will become [**The Gardeners' House**](https://www.thegardenershouse.org/) - a new learning centre for botanical and horticultural projects, a natural history reading room, lecture hall, open-air laboratory for ‘citizen science’ and communal activity spaces.

In 2016 the Hypatia Trust donated £15,000 to get a team together to do the initial feasibility study. This seedcorn money was part of a bequest to the Trust from a supporter in the USA.

In November 2017 the team obtained a [**Heritage Lottery Fund**](https://www.hlf.org.uk/) grant of £70,000 to complete the planning and development stages. We are now trying to raise £100,000 to add to the fund, demonstrating that supporters locally and internationally are backing the project. If all this can be done successfully, it will hopefully unlock a further £800,000 grant from the Heritage Lottery Fund to carry out the extensive building work. With further fundraising and additional grants the total budget of about £1.2 million will be achieved.

[**The Gardeners' House**](https://www.thegardenershouse.org) will provide educational and training opportunities and house the [**Hypatia Natural History collection**](http://hypatia-trust.org.uk/hypatias-collections/) – a special collection and women’s archive – books, papers and ephemera showing the engagement of women in recording the botanical, horticultural, photographic and artistic nature of the Cornish landscape.  It will also create a warm and welcoming space for clients of the Pengarth Day Centre, a home for  [**The Friends of Morrab Gardens**](http://www.morrabgardens.org/the-friends-of-morrab-gardens/) and benefit the whole town of Penzance and Cornish communities as well as its wider botanical and horticultural national and international visitors.

This is a really big project for a small town like Penzance, and will provide benefits for years to come.
