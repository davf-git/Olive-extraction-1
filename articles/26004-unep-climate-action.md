### Climate action

### UNEP provides evidence-based data to inform policy decisions, collaborates with sectors for low-carbon transitions, provides climate finance mechanisms and aids countries in adaptation and mitigation efforts.

# 

# The world is in a climate emergency. Unless greenhouse gas emissions fall dramatically, warming could pass 2.9°C this century

Unless greenhouse gas emissions fall dramatically, warming could pass 2.9°C this century, which would have catastrophic consequences for life on this planet. UNEP takes a four-pronged approach to addressing the climate crisis and reducing greenhouse gas emissions in line with the Paris Agreement. The organisation;

**→** provides [cutting-edge research](https://www.unep.org/publications-data) to support science-based decision-making on climate change

**→** works [across sectors](https://www.unep.org/topics/climate-action/mitigation) to support the transition to a low-carbon, climate-resilient future

**→** ensures a just transition to a carbon-neutral world by empowering communities [to adapt to changing climatic conditions](https://www.unep.org/topics/climate-action/adaptation); and

**→** develops sustainable mechanisms to [unlock financing](https://www.unep.org/topics/climate-action/climate-finance) to help countries both mitigate and adapt to climate change.
