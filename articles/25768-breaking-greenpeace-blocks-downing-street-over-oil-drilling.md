# **Breaking: Greenpeace blocks Downing Street over oil drilling**

Greenpeace activists shutdown Downing Street with an oil soaked statue of Prime Minister Boris Johnson.

* 11TH OCTOBER 2021
* [OIL DRILLING AND PIPELINES](https://www.greenpeace.org.uk/latest-updates/?category=oil-drilling-and-pipelines)
* [BLOG](https://www.greenpeace.org.uk/latest-updates/?news_type=blog)

### **Across the world and here in the UK, our dependence on fossil fuels is causing havoc – [deadly floods and heat waves](https://www.greenpeace.org.uk/challenges/climate-change/effects-climate-change/), [rising gas prices](https://www.greenpeace.org.uk/news/uk-energy-crisis-gas-dependence/), queues at petrol pumps, [offshore workers in and out of precarious work](https://www.greenpeace.org.uk/news/rigged-a-workers-story/). We need to get off fossil fuels and transition to renewable energy.**

But Boris Johnson has said he backs 16 new North Sea oil and gas projects going ahead – including [Cambo](https://www.greenpeace.org.uk/news/cambo-chaos-oil-embarrassing-government-ahead-cop-climate-talks/). They could be approved at any moment. It’s time for our over-reliance on fossil fuels to end.
