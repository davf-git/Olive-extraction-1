# **Systems Change Explained: What It Is and How It Works**

29/07/2026

What does systems change actually mean? Watch the fourth video in our global commons e[xplainer series](https://globalcommonsalliance.org/what-are-global-commons/), to find out what systems change means, why it’s urgent, and how it actually happens in practice.   
   
 Our current systems weren’t built for a planet under this much pressure.   
 But transforming our relationship with nature isn’t about one sector acting alone. Systems change requires:   
 – new values of collaborative stewardship and responsibility,   
 – governance that’s transparent and accountable,   
 – economies that work within planetary boundaries, and   
 – science and lived experience guiding decisions together.   
   
 Above all, it means centering the people most affected by environmental and social change, and moving fast, together.   
   
 Watch our latest video to find out what systems change looks like in practice – and join us in building it.
