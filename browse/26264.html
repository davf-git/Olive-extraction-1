Voices for now for tomorrow

IF WE CAN HEAR VOICES OF REASON, EVERYTHING CAN BE POSSIBLE

By sharing progressive ideas and long-term thinking the aim of the Olive Network is to connect populations with positive change makers, rising above division to the empathy and vision of charities, the arts, science and humanities.

Our aim is to create a bridge from the peopleworking hard to make things rightto the people who are looking for answers.

In the 'age of the screen' and rapid disinformation the challenge is to amplify voices of reason so that populations can hear them and know there can be a better way. Our belief is that if people could actually see the progress that is possible they would embrace the hope they find within it.

In this video, hear the voices of Volker Türk, Elif Shafak, Omar El Akaad, Greta Thunberg, Pope Francis, Wim Wenders, Agnès Callamard, Brian Eno, Sage Lenier, Jason Hickel and Francesca Albanese.
