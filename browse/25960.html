Featured Story: We have established the lesson for the entire world.

Beautiful Words - simple as that! (ON Ed)

we have

demonstrated that it

is possible for people

coming from all lands

and climes of the

world...to live together

as good neighbours, in

peace and harmony. if

we can do it here, it can

be done elsewhere

Fiorello la Guardia 1938

Sabine Hornig, La Guardia Vistas (2020). Commissioned by LaGuardia Gateway Partners in partnership with Public Art Fund for LaGuardia Airport’s Terminal B. Photo: Nicholas Knight, courtesy of the artist; LaGuardia Gateway Partners; Public Art Fund, NY; and Tanya Bonakdar Gallery, New York/Los Angeles. ©Sabine Hornig and VG Bild-Kunst, Bonn, Germany.
