ART-2030 CONNECTING ART AND THE UN GLOBAL GOALS

Mission

ART 2030 works with art as the key to achieve the UN Global Goals by opening people's hearts, minds and imagination - to inspire action for a healthy and sustainable future.

Working with world renowned artists and partners, we facilitate exciting art projects connected to the UN Global Goals - including public events, art experiences, multi-platform communication, and educational activities - for all to engage with the plan for people, planet and prosperity.

ART 2030 is a non-profit organization uniting art with the United Nations 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development and its 17 Global Goals.

ART 2030 was founded by Luise Faurschou, with support from founding partners Torben Wind and John Blem, and with a generous first donation from the Hempel Foundation.

ART 2030 is very thankful for the support of Roskilde Festival, Statens Kunstfond, Code Art Fair, and World Climate Ltd.

In addition ART 2030 is very grateful for all donations, pro bono services and support by others to our mission.
